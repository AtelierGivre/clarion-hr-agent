# Remote Work Policy

Applies to all employees. Effective from 1 April 2026. Owned by People Operations.

## Default working model

GoAified is remote first. Employees may work from home, from a coworking space, or from a GoAified office. There is no fixed number of office days. Teams may agree on shared days for collaboration, and those agreements are recorded by the team lead.

## Eligibility

All employees are eligible for remote work from day one. Interns are eligible only for work from home, unless their offer letter says otherwise.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 India time. Outside core hours, you may arrange your day as you prefer, as long as you attend your meetings and meet your commitments. Teams that serve clients in other time zones may set different core hours with manager approval.

## Workspace setup

The company provides a laptop and a one time home office allowance of 15,000 rupees for a desk or chair. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of joining. Internet costs are not reimbursed. A stable connection of at least 20 Mbps is expected.

## Working from another city or country

You may work from another city in India for up to 30 days per year without approval. Inform your manager in the HR portal before you travel.

Working from outside India requires approval from your manager and from People Operations at least 15 working days in advance, because of tax and data rules. The maximum is 30 days per year.

## Data and security while remote

Use only company managed devices for company work. Do not use public computers or shared devices. Follow the IT Security Policy for VPN use, screen privacy, and approved tools. When on a video call in a public place, use headphones and avoid discussing client data.

## Attendance and availability

Remote work is not a substitute for availability. Keep your calendar updated, respond to messages during core hours, and join meetings on time with your camera on when the meeting is internal and client facing.

## Office access

Employees who visit a GoAified office must book a desk in the workspace app at least one day in advance. Visitor passes are arranged by the office coordinator.
