# Code of Conduct

Applies to all employees, interns, and contractors. Effective from 1 April 2026. Owned by People Operations.

## Our standard

Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Disagree with ideas, not with people. Be direct and kind. Assume good intent and ask before judging.

## Harassment and discrimination

Harassment of any kind is not tolerated. This includes unwelcome comments about a person's body, caste, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or age. Discrimination in hiring, pay, or promotion is a serious violation.

## Reporting a concern

Report a concern to your manager, your People Partner, or the internal committee. You may also write to conduct@goaified.example. Reports are handled confidentially. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is itself a violation.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose outside work, paid advisory roles, and family relationships inside the company to People Operations. Do not use your position to benefit a relative or a business you own.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept small gifts up to 2,000 rupees in value. Anything above that must be disclosed and handed to People Operations. Never accept cash or cash equivalents from a vendor or client.

## Confidentiality

Client work, internal plans, and pricing are confidential. Do not discuss them in public, on social media, or with friends outside the company. The confidentiality obligation continues after you leave.

## Use of company name

Do not speak to the press or post publicly on behalf of GoAified unless you are authorised by the founder. Sharing that you work at GoAified is fine.

## Disciplinary process

Violations are reviewed by People Operations. Outcomes range from a written warning to termination, depending on severity. You will be given a chance to respond before a decision is made.
