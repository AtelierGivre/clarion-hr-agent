# IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy

Applies to all employees, interns, and contractors. Effective from 1 April 2026. Owned by the IT team.

## Scope

This policy covers company laptops, company accounts, client data, and any tool used for company work, including personal devices when they hold company data.

## Passwords and multi factor authentication

Use a password manager. Every company account must use a unique password of at least 14 characters. Multi factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal, GitHub, cloud consoles, and any tool that holds client data. SMS based MFA is allowed only when no app based option exists.

## Devices

Company work must happen on a company managed laptop with disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes. Personal laptops may be used only for reading email. Do not install unapproved software. Software requests go through the IT helpdesk and are reviewed within 3 working days.

## Approved AI tools

Only tools approved by IT may be used with company or client data. The current approved list is Google Gemini through the company workspace, GitHub Copilot, and Claude through the approved enterprise account.

Free consumer versions of AI tools, including the public ChatGPT website, are not approved for company or client data. You may use them only for public information with no client content. Never paste client code, documents, customer data, or credentials into an unapproved tool.

## Client data handling

Client data must stay in systems approved for that client. Do not copy client data to personal drives, personal email, or unapproved cloud accounts. Do not use client data to train or fine tune any model. When a client requires a dedicated environment, follow the project runbook.

## Phishing and suspicious email

Report suspected phishing using the Report Phishing button in Gmail, or forward the message to security@goaified.example. Do not click links or open attachments in a message you suspect. The IT team will confirm within 4 working hours. If you entered credentials on a suspicious page, change your password immediately and tell IT.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 1 hour. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. Do not delay the report to try to find the device yourself.

## Access reviews

Access to client systems is reviewed every quarter. Access that is no longer needed is removed. Departing employees lose access on their last working day.

## Incidents

Report any security incident, including a wrong email recipient or a leaked key, within 1 hour to security@goaified.example. Early reporting is always treated as good behaviour. Hiding an incident is a disciplinary matter.
