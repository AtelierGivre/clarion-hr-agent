# Leave and Time Off Policy

Applies to all full time employees of GoAified. Effective from 1 April 2026. Owned by People Operations.

## Leave year

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. All balances reset on 1 April. Balances are shown in the HR portal and are updated within one working day of an approved request.

## Types of leave

GoAified offers three kinds of paid leave.

Casual leave: 12 days per year. One day is credited at the start of each month. Casual leave is meant for short personal needs and cannot be carried into the next leave year.

Sick leave: 10 days per year. Sick leave can be used for your own illness or for the illness of a dependent. A medical certificate is required when sick leave is taken for more than 3 consecutive working days.

Earned leave: 18 days per year. Earned leave is credited at 1.5 days per month. Up to 30 unused earned leave days can be carried forward into the next leave year. Any balance above 30 days lapses on 31 March.

## Public holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays each year. The holiday calendar is published in the HR portal every January. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute day is given.

## How to request leave

All leave must be applied for in the HR portal. Requests sent by email or chat are not valid.

For planned leave of 2 days or less, apply at least 3 working days in advance.

For planned leave of 3 days or more, apply at least 10 working days in advance.

For unplanned sick leave, inform your manager before 10:00 on the first day and apply in the portal within 48 hours.

Approval is given by your reporting manager. Leave is confirmed only after approval. Do not book travel before the request is approved.

## Leave during probation

Employees in their first 90 days can use sick leave from day one. Casual leave and earned leave are available after the first 30 days, on a pro rata basis.

## Working on a leave day

If you are contacted for work during approved leave, you may claim a compensatory day off. Compensatory leave must be used within 60 days and needs manager approval.

## Unused leave at exit

On resignation, unused earned leave is paid out at your basic salary rate. Casual leave and sick leave are not paid out. Notice period rules are covered in the employment contract.

## Questions

For leave balance corrections, raise a ticket with People Operations in the HR portal. For questions about this policy, contact your People Partner.
