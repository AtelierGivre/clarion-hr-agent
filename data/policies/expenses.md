# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

Applies to all employees and interns. Effective from 1 April 2026. Owned by Finance.

## General rules

Spend company money only when it is needed for work and is reasonable. When in doubt, ask your manager before you spend. Every claim needs an itemised receipt. Claims without a receipt are rejected, except for local travel under 200 rupees.

## Submission window

Submit claims in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense date. Claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle, which runs on the 28th of each month.

## Travel

Book flights and trains through the company travel desk. Economy class is the default. A train journey under 6 hours should be taken by train rather than air.

Hotel limits per night: 6,000 rupees in Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru, and 4,000 rupees in other cities. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 1,200 rupees per day.

Local transport to and from the airport or station is reimbursed with receipts. Use of personal vehicles is reimbursed at 12 rupees per kilometre.

## Client entertainment

Client meals need prior manager approval above 5,000 rupees. The claim must list the client name, the attendees, and the business purpose.

## Home office allowance

A one time allowance of 15,000 rupees is available for a desk or chair. Submit receipts within 60 days of joining. Internet and electricity are not reimbursed.

## Learning and certifications

Employees may claim up to 25,000 rupees per year for courses and certifications related to their role. Manager approval is needed before purchase. Exam retake fees are not covered.

## What is not reimbursed

Alcohol, personal entertainment, gym memberships, fines and penalties, personal shopping, and upgrades on flights or hotels. Gifts to clients above 2,000 rupees need approval from Finance.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards are issued to team leads and above. Card statements are reconciled within 7 days of the statement date. Personal spending on a corporate card must be repaid within 15 days.
