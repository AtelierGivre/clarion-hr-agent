How it works

  1. 1
    Reads the policy

    Your question becomes a vector search across the policy library in Postgres.

  2. 2
    Checks your records

    For personal questions, the agent calls an MCP tool that can only read your row.

  3. 3
    Guards the answer

    Injection attempts are blocked, personal data is redacted, and policy claims need a source.
